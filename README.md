# 🏪 Kajal Digital Seva Kendra

### Digital Services Made Simple

A modern, responsive website for **Kajal Digital Seva Kendra (Kajal Store)**, a local Digital Seva Centre providing government, educational, employment, documentation, printing, banking and digital payment assistance.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://csc-pragya-kendra.vercel.app/

---

## ✨ Features

- 📱 Fully responsive design
- 🏠 Professional home page
- 🛠️ Complete digital service listing
- 🏛️ Government & ID service assistance
- 🎓 Education & student services
- 💼 Job & employment services
- 📝 Online application assistance
- 🖨️ Print, photocopy, scanning & lamination services
- 💳 UPI payment section
- 📲 WhatsApp integration
- 📄 Online service application forms
- 📍 Google Maps location
- 📞 Contact section
- 📧 Contact form
- 🔗 Easy navigation between all pages
- ⚡ Optimized website assets
- 🚀 Production deployment with Vercel
